# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want iSystem OS to be a useful, welcoming project for people with different backgrounds, identities, abilities, experience levels, and viewpoints. Participation should be safe, respectful, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, and boundaries.
- Give credit and disclose meaningful conflicts of interest.
- Accept moderation decisions and make space for others.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, stalking, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Neither is using project spaces to promote fraud, abuse, or unlawful access.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces, project events, and public situations where someone is representing iSystem OS or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary moderation, use a private maintainer contact through the repository. If a report includes a security vulnerability, use a private Security Advisory as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban people when needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

This project does not promise a fixed response time during beta. If immediate personal safety is at risk, contact appropriate local services rather than relying on project maintainers.
