# Authors and attribution

iSystem OS was created and is stewarded by [AfifiNL](https://github.com/AfifiNL) under the iSystem.ai brand.

The first public release is a clean, security-reviewed source snapshot rather than a publication of the private development history. This boundary protects customer and operational material; it is not intended to erase authorship. Contributors to the public repository are recorded in its Git history and may also be acknowledged in release notes.

Original contributions are accepted under Apache-2.0 as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Third-party authorship and asset provenance are recorded in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and [docs/asset-provenance.md](docs/asset-provenance.md).
